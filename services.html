What We Offer

Seven Services. One Team.

A complete outsourced sales operation for your studio — from the first call to the signed membership.

01
Outbound Call Drives
High-volume daily outbound calling
What it is
High-volume daily outbound calling to leads assigned to your studio.
What we do
Our team runs structured call drives against your lead list every day. We follow a proven cadence built around your program — not a generic script. 1,400+ calls per location per month.
What you get

More contacts, more conversations, more enrollments.

02
Lead Follow-Up & Nurture
Full pipeline lead management
What it is
Full management of your inbound and pipeline leads.
What we do
Every new lead is contacted within 5 minutes of submission. We manage the full follow-up sequence — calls, texts, CRM notes — through to conversion or close.
What you get

No lead left uncontacted. No opportunity lost to slow response.

03
Membership Sales & Enrollment
Full sales conversations from needs assessment to close
What it is
Full sales conversations from needs assessment to close.
What we do
We conduct needs assessments, build value in your specific program, handle pricing, and close memberships — primarily on the first call. Trial class is the backup, not the default.
What you get

Higher first-call close rates and fewer leads that go cold after a trial.

04
Appointment Setting & Confirmation
Trial class scheduling, confirmation & no-show follow-up
What it is
Trial class scheduling, confirmation, and no-show follow-up.
What we do
We book trial classes, confirm attendance in advance, and follow up immediately on no-shows to rebook. Every appointment is tracked and managed.
What you get

Higher trial show rates and faster rebooking when someone misses.

05
CRM Management
Full lead and pipeline management in your CRM
What it is
Full lead and pipeline management inside your CRM.
What we do
All activity is logged in real time. Leads are tagged, notes are detailed, tasks are set, and pipelines are kept current. Studio staff always have complete visibility.
What you get

A clean, accurate CRM that actually reflects reality — not a graveyard of old leads.

06
Campaign Execution
Targeted outreach campaigns for your studio's specific needs
What it is
Targeted outreach campaigns built around your studio's specific needs.
What we do
We design and run campaigns for 10-week challenge enrollment, reactivation of cold members, referral drives, founding member outreach, holiday promotions, and more. All campaigns are discussed and approved with ownership before launch.
What you get

Strategic lead generation that goes beyond the standard call list.

07
Daily EOD Reporting + Monthly MACRO
Full daily and monthly reporting delivered to ownership
What it is
Full daily and monthly reporting delivered to ownership.
What we do
Every location receives a daily EOD report covering calls made, daily sales, monthly running totals, appointments, leads generated, follow-ups, and notes. At month-end, we deliver a full MACRO report covering overall performance, campaign results, conversion trends, and strategic recommendations. We also hold biweekly check-in calls with each franchise owner.
What you get

Complete daily and monthly visibility into your sales operation without having to chase anyone for updates.
