Our Story

Built from the inside out

By people who have actually done this work.

Cameron La Guerre
Founder & CEO

Cameron La Guerre founded Aphani Solutions after building his sales career from the inside of boutique fitness programs — FXB locations in Texas and Colorado, The Max Challenge on the East Coast, F45 Training, and Mayweather Boxing & Fitness. He didn't just observe these programs — he worked them, sold them, and learned exactly where the leads went to die.

He founded Aphani Solutions with one mission: build a sales team that understands the product, believes in the programs, and closes like it.

Today, Cameron personally leads the call center methodology used across all 16 active Aphani locations, manages all client relationships, and oversees performance standards and sales strategy across four states.

  • 16 active FXB locations currently under management — MN, IA, NE, and AZ
  • Cross-brand experience: FXB (TX, CO, Midwest), The Max Challenge, F45, Mayweather Boxing & Fitness
  • Personally leads all call center methodology and client strategy
  • Manages all client relationships and performance standards
